FaidaHQ Privacy Policy

FaidaHQ ("we," "us," or "our") operates the FaidaHQ Business Operating System, including our web applications, mobile applications, USSD integrations, automated tools, and AI services. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you interact with our platform.

1. Overview & Scope

Whether you run a retail shop, mobile business, service enterprise, or tech organization, FaidaHQ provides tools for managing sales, inventory, invoicing, team collaboration, USSD services, and AI automation. We understand that your business operational data is confidential and vital.

This policy applies to all users, account administrators, and visitors across all FaidaHQ domains, mobile applications, and connected services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in three primary categories:

A. Account & Contact Information
  • Profile Data: Name, phone number, email address, business location, and account credentials.
  • Business Identification: Business name, tax ID (where applicable), industry type, and contact preferences.
B. Business Operational Data
  • Sales & Inventory Records: Product catalogs, pricing, quantity levels, sales transactions, receipts, customer records, and supplier details.
  • Financial Entries: Expense tracking, invoice histories, debt logs, and cash flow records processed through FaidaHQ modules.
  • Communication & Support Data: Messages submitted via contact forms, support tickets, and email inquiries.
C. Technical, Offline & Device Data
  • Device & Network Identifiers: IP address, browser type, device hardware parameters, operating system, and system performance metrics.
  • Offline Storage & Caching: Local data stored on your browser or device (such as IndexedDB/localStorage) to support uninterrupted offline business operations.
  • AI & Automation Interactions: Queries, parameters, and input context submitted to FaidaHQ's AI agent and automated workflow triggers.

3. How We Use Information

We utilize the collected information strictly for legitimate operational and business purposes, including:

Platform Delivery

Providing real-time inventory management, sales tracking, invoice generation, and multi-channel synchronization.

Offline Capability

Ensuring seamless local offline data entry and automatic background synchronization when reconnecting to the internet.

AI & Analytics

Generating automated insights, intelligent stock level recommendations, and business performance summaries.

Security & Compliance

Detecting unauthorized access, enforcing authentication protocols, and complying with statutory obligations.

4. Data Security & Offline Sync

Security is embedded into the core architecture of FaidaHQ:

  • Data Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our cloud servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS (HTTPS). Sensitive stored fields are encrypted at rest.
  • Offline Storage Security: Data cached locally on your device for offline operation remains on your local hardware until synchronized. We advise users to maintain device lock protection (PIN/biometrics).
  • Access Controls: Multi-tenant isolation ensures that your business operational data is accessible solely by authorized personnel under your account.

5. Third-Party Data Sharing

FaidaHQ DOES NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal or business operational data to third parties for advertising or marketing.

We share data only under the following limited conditions:

  1. Trusted Service Providers: Secure cloud hosting infrastructure, SMS/USSD telecommunication gateways, and email dispatch services bound by strict contractual confidentiality agreements.
  2. Legal Requirements: If mandated by court order, law enforcement request, or statutory regulation within Kenya or other applicable jurisdiction.
  3. Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or restructuring, where data handling obligations remain governed by this Privacy Policy.

6. Your Data Rights & Choices

Subject to applicable data protection legislation, you retain full ownership and rights over your data:

  • Access & Export: You can download or export your sales, inventory, and customer data in standard formats at any time.
  • Correction & Updating: Modify incorrect account or business details directly within your settings dashboard.
  • Data Erasure: Request permanent deletion of your account and associated operational records by contacting our data support team.
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from non-essential promotional communications via the link in our emails or account preferences.

7. Cookies & Local Web Storage

We use essential cookies and web storage technologies (session tokens, localStorage, IndexedDB) to enable key platform features:

  • Maintaining user authentication and security tokens during active sessions.
  • Storing user interface preferences and offline operational data cache.
  • Analyzing aggregated, anonymous platform usage metrics to improve application responsiveness.

8. Regulatory Compliance

FaidaHQ operates in compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (KDPA) and aligns with international data protection principles. We uphold the rights of data subjects regarding lawful processing, data minimization, accuracy, and storage limitation.
